Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

In short: your documents stay on your device and in your own iCloud account. We cannot see them. The app collects only anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off in Settings.

Who we are

Doki is developed by the Doki team (“we”). Contact: to be added before release.

Your documents

  • Documents, scans, numbers, dates, names and notes you add are stored on your device and synced through Apple iCloud (CloudKit) in your own private iCloud account. Sensitive fields are stored with iCloud encryption.
  • We do not operate servers that receive your documents, and we have no access to your iCloud data.
  • Text recognition (scanning) runs on your device. Images and recognized text are not sent to us or to third parties.
  • If you share documents with family members, they are shared through Apple iCloud with the people you invite. You can stop sharing at any time.

Anonymous usage statistics

To understand how the app is used and improve it, Doki sends anonymous events through TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH, Germany). Events contain only technical values such as the type of screen or action, document category, ranges like “3–5 documents”, app version, device model, system language and region, and the country of residence at country level. They never contain document contents, numbers, names, dates of documents, photos or your contact details.

The user identifier is an anonymous value that is hashed on your device and again on the server; it is not linked to your Apple ID, e-mail or identity, and it is not used for tracking across apps or websites. You can turn statistics off at any time in Settings → Privacy.

Settings and updates

From time to time the app downloads a shared settings file and information about renewal periods from the Doki website on GitHub Pages. The request does not contain your data. GitHub, as the hosting provider, can see your IP address.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive only the status of your subscription (for example, active or expired) from Apple, never your payment details.

Notifications, Face ID and camera

  • Expiry reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.
  • Face ID / passcode protection uses Apple’s system authentication; we never receive biometric data.
  • The camera is used only to scan documents when you choose to.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad tracking, no selling or sharing of data with data brokers.
  • No account or registration required.

Data retention and deletion

Your documents are kept until you delete them in the app or delete the app’s data from iCloud (iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage). Anonymous statistics are kept by TelemetryDeck in aggregated form and cannot be linked back to you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete data and to object to processing. Because we do not hold your documents or identifying information, most requests can be fulfilled directly in the app. For anything else, contact us at to be added before release.

Children

Doki can store documents of family members, including children, added by an adult. The app is not directed at children under 13 for independent use.

Information about renewal rules

Renewal periods and links to official websites are provided for convenience and may be out of date. Always check the official source. This is not legal advice.

Changes

We will update this page and the effective date if this policy changes.
